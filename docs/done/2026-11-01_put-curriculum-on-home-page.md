# Put the curriculum on the home page

- **Opened:** 2026-10-31, by the user.
- **Status:** in progress since 2026-10-31.

## Why

The site's only page says "Sito in costruzione". Before the site moves
to its own domain ([card](../backlog/choose-site-address.md)), it should show real
content, so the clean address is first indexed on something concrete and
not on a site under construction. The author's curriculum is that
content: the user already has it in Markdown, and it needs nothing else
around it.

The curriculum is a page of its own, not an article (decided by the user
on 2026-10-31), so the choice of the articles' licence
([card](../backlog/choose-articles-licence.md)) still comes before the first
article, not before this card.

The site stays one page, with no internal links. Later, once the two
articles already on Medium are on the site, the home page becomes the
list of contents and the menu arrives
([card](../backlog/add-site-navigation.md)).
