- **Opened:** 2026-11-14, by the user, while deciding the style guide's
  navigation in the card
  [link the style guide's pages to its index](../done/2026-11-14_link-the-style-guide-pages-to-its-index.md);

- **Status:** noticed, not started.

## Why

The style guide's pages get a breadcrumb at the top, leading to the
style guide's index, and a link at the bottom that leads back to the top
of the page. The user decided to give the articles the same: they are
the longest pages of the site, so the way back to the top serves them
more than the style guide, and a breadcrumb tells the reader where an
article sits.

## Known from the start

- **The style guide comes first:** the breadcrumb and the way back to
  the top are built there, and this card reuses them.
- **The site's navigation is the menu's job**
  ([card](add-site-menu.md)): a breadcrumb shows where a page sits, it
  does not lead around the site.

## Questions

- What an article's breadcrumb leads to: the site has no page listing
  the articles, they are listed on the home page, which the logo
  already leads to.
- Whether the curriculum, also a long page, gets the way back to the
  top too.
