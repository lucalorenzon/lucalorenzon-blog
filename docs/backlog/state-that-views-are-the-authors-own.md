# State that the views are the author's own

- **Opened:** 2026-11-03, by the user, from the card
  [show the licences on the site](../done/2026-11-03_show-licences-on-the-site.md).
- **Status:** noticed, not started. In Next.

## Why

The author is employed, and writes about technologies and problems met
at work, past and present. A statement such as "the views here are my
own, not my employer's" is common for authors in that position: it
tells readers, and employers, that an article speaks for the author
only. It came up while choosing the name of the licence page: on Simon
Willison's and Martin Fowler's sites, "Disclosures" is where such
statements live, together with who pays them and what they own.

## Known from the start

- **The licence page holds the licences only**, by the user's choice
  of 2026-11-02; its name, `licence.html`, was chosen over
  "Disclosure" for that reason.
- **The articles are the trigger.** The first two, in Now, describe a
  platform the author worked on for past employers; the statement
  matters most once they are live.
- **The repository is public:** neither the statement nor the articles
  may hold employer internals.

## Questions

- The wording, and whether it names the employer or stays generic.
- Where it lives: on every page, at the end of each article, or on a
  page of its own (a "Disclosure" page, linked from the footer), and
  how it sits beside the footer's licence link.
- Whether anything else belongs in it (sponsorships, affiliations),
  now or later.
- Whether it comes before the first articles are published.
