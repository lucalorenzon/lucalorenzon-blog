## Why

As articles and other pages arrive, the reader needs an easy way to
move around the site: a tool that offers the actions available to
them. The user wants this card to state the need, not a form for it.

The header already leaves room for it: on every width it holds the
site's identity on the left and keeps space for this tool on the right
([card](../done/2026-10-29_adapt-header-to-screen-width.md)).

On 2026-11-06 the user chose to publish the articles first with what
the site already has: the logo leading to the home page, the list of
articles there, the footer's link to the about page
([card](../done/2026-11-06_list-articles-on-home-page.md)). This card
brings the visible tool, and the actions beyond finding and reading
the articles.

## Entries the user has in mind

Listed by the user on 2026-10-31, when doing the menu before the
articles was considered and then dropped. They are a starting point for
the first question below, not a decision:

- Search.
- About me, leading to the author's curriculum.
- Categories: Product Development, Architecture, AI, E-Commerce.
- Subscribe.
- Settings: dark or light theme; menu on the right or on the left. The
  light theme and the menu position would each get a card of their own.

An entry that does not work yet can be shown disabled, to say the
feature is coming: screen readers announce a disabled control as
unavailable, so it does not mislead.

## When to pick this up

After the version that publishes the
[list of articles on the home page](../done/2026-11-06_list-articles-on-home-page.md).

## Questions

- Which actions the reader needs, and which come first.
- How the tool looks and behaves on large and small screens.
