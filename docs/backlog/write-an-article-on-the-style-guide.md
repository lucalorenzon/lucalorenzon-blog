# Write an article on the style guide

- **Opened:** 2026-11-10, by the user, while choosing where the colour
  values of the style guide are defined, in the card
  [build a design system](../wip/build-a-design-system.md); in Next,
  after the style guide's colour page is live.
- **Status:** noticed, not started.

## Why

The style guide says what: the colours, the fonts, how each component
behaves, with a short reason for each. It has no room for why and how
it came to be, which is what an article can tell: a personal site with
two articles that ends up with a style guide; the browser tests that
depended on the articles; Holt measured and rejected for its maturity;
the colours defined once in the build, which writes the stylesheet from
them; the contrasts computed and checked by the build. Told from its
reason, the article also keeps the style guide in proportion to the
size of the site.

## Known from the start

- **Not while the style guide is built:** in the session that opened
  this card, the catalogue went from Holt to a style guide of our own;
  an article written alongside would be rewritten at every turn. It is
  written once the colour page is live.
- **The material is already recorded:** the card on the design system
  and the session minutes keep the decisions and their reasons, and are
  the article's outline.
- **The tone:** it starts from the problem, the tests tied to the
  content, not from the style guide itself.
