# Hide the header's line under the logo's name

- **Created:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** In work

## Why

Cut by the user on 2026-11-22 from the card
[review the logo](review-the-logo.md), which builds first the icon and
the name in Literata bold italic with its baseline on the header's
line, without this effect.

The effect was the user's idea in the session that designed the logo:
under the name the line disappears, like a thread that runs inside the
letters. It reaches the foot of the "L", is left only where each
letter touches it, and comes out at the foot of the "r". Without it,
the line runs under the whole name, and the logo stands on it instead
of being woven into it.

Taken into work on 2026-11-22, right after the logo's card was closed:
on the screenshots of the real header, the user approved everything
but the line that runs on under the letters of the name.

## What is known

- **The effect was tried** by the agent on 2026-11-22, in a copy of the
  built site served on the user's machine (`target/logo-look/mock-site/`,
  not committed; `target/` is wiped by a clean build). The stretch of
  line hidden ran from the first to the last pixel the letters ink just
  above the baseline, measured on a canvas in the same font. The user
  confirmed it was the effect intended.
- **How the trial hid the line** (read by the agent on 2026-11-22 in
  `target/logo-look/mock-site/logo-mock.js`): a script in the page drew
  the name on a canvas, found the two points on the row of pixels one
  pixel above the baseline, moved them by the 5 degrees of extra slant,
  and laid over the line a band 4 pixels high, in the colour of the
  page, behind the letters, so the letters stayed drawn over it. The
  site has no script today: done for real, the band's ends must come
  from somewhere else.
- **The line is drawn by two different boxes** (checked by the agent
  on 2026-11-22 in `style/input.css` and `src/page_frame.rs`): below
  1280 pixels it is the top border of the title (`title-below-line`),
  from 1280 up the bottom border of the header (`header-one-row`). The
  name sits on it in both forms (`logo-name`, in the link `logo-link`).
- **What the user approved on the screenshots** of the slice
  (`target/logo-look/shots/real-*.png`, not committed): the icon and
  the name on the line as they are; the only remark was the line under
  the letters, which is this card.
- **Those two points depend on the font and its size**, and the name
  grows with the title, from 24 to 32 pixels. Done for real, either the
  build computes them from Literata's measures, as it does for the
  tables' widths (`src/table_widths.rs`, from `src/literata_widths.rs`),
  or a browser test checks them.
- **The measures of the fonts may move into the build**: the card
  [take the measures from the configured fonts](take-the-measures-from-the-configured-fonts.md)
  would take them in the browser instead of from widths generated by
  hand.

## Questions to answer

The ones known when the card was written; there may be more.

- **How the two points are found**: computed by the build, checked by a
  browser test, or both.

  **Answered by the user on 2026-11-22.** Neither: no point is found.
  Asked by the agent where the knowledge of the two points should
  live, the user asked whether CSS could intersect two elements, or
  mask the line with the name in the page's colour. The agent answered
  that CSS has no intersection of two elements (`clip-path` takes
  shapes, `mask` images and gradients, `element()` works in Firefox
  only), and tried three ways on the real header, with the style added
  in the browser to the built site (`target/thread-look/`, not
  committed), at 375 and 1366 pixels, dark and light, at double pixel
  density:
  - *the name in negative*, a ring of the page's colour around each
    letter (`text-shadow`, or a stroke painted under the letters): short
    dashes of line stay between the letters, and the ring cuts the line
    off the feet of the "L" and of the "r";
  - *the name's box* in the page's colour, behind the letters: the line
    disappears under the whole name and reaches the foot of the "L",
    but comes back 8 to 10 pixels after the foot of the "r", whose
    italic stroke reaches further right at the top than at the foot,
    where the box ends;
  - *the box trimmed*: the same colour stopping 0.32 times the size of
    the letters before the box's right end
    (`linear-gradient` of the page's colour, `background-size:
    calc(100% - 0.32em) 100%`): the line comes back at the foot of the
    "r", measured on the screenshots at both widths and in both themes,
    and looks like the trial with a script.

  The user chose the box trimmed. No script and no measure in the
  build; its price is the one number, 0.32, which depends on the "r" of
  Literata bold italic: a change of the role `logo`'s font, or of the
  name's last letter, needs it measured again.
- **What happens when the font of the role `logo` changes**: whether
  the build stops, as it does when a code font moves the wrap toggle's
  threshold
  ([measure the fonts again when they change](measure-the-fonts-again-when-they-change.md)).

  **Proposed by the agent on 2026-11-22, not answered yet.** With the
  box trimmed, a browser test that checks the line touching the feet of
  the "L" and of the "r" would fail, and stop the pipeline, when the
  font or the name changes.

  **Answered by the user on 2026-11-22.** The test in the pipeline is
  enough; the tie between the number and the font is not written
  anywhere else. The agent had named the weakness of a check made on
  pixels: Chromium on Linux already drew a font a whole number of
  pixels wide, against the build's measures.

- **What happens in forced colours mode**, where the system imposes
  its own colours. Raised and checked by the agent on 2026-11-22, in
  Chromium only, the one browser of the project's tests: the
  gradient is dropped, but Chromium draws a band of the system's
  background behind every text, which already hides the line under the
  name today, without this card; the effect stays, at most with a gap
  after the "r". Firefox was not checked.

## Acceptance tests

Agreed with the user on 2026-11-22. Each one names how it is checked.

The line under the name:

1. On every page, at every width of the browser tests' list of screens
   and in both themes, the header's line reaches the foot of the "L"
   and comes back at the foot of the "r", within one pixel. *Browser
   test, on the line's pixels.*
2. Between the foot of the "L" and the foot of the "r", the line does
   not show between the letters. *The same browser test.*
3. Nothing else changes: outside the name the line is whole, the
   stretch between the icon and the "L" included, and the name's
   background covers neither the icon nor the title's text below the
   line. *The header's browser tests already there, and a check on the
   background.*

A change of font:

4. Proof that test 1 notices a change: once, outside the commit, the
   role `logo` is moved to another font and the test is seen to fail;
   the outcome is written in this card, as for the wrap toggle's
   threshold
   ([measure the fonts again when they change](measure-the-fonts-again-when-they-change.md)).

Before the commit:

5. The user sees screenshots of the real header on a narrow screen and
   on a laptop, in both themes, and approves them. *Check by eye.*
6. The pipeline's checks pass: formatting, clippy, unit tests, type
   check, browser tests; the row of `end2end/README.md` follows the
   test. *Automatic.*
