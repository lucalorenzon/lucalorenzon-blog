Every page of this site sits in the same frame: a header with the logo and the page's title, a column for the text, and a footer with the terms of reuse. This page is in the frame too, so what it describes is all around what you are reading. Make the window narrower or wider and you will see it change.

## The header

The logo stands on a line that runs across the page: a round photo, and the name Lvk@73r beside it. The line passes under the name like a thread, hidden behind the letters. On the right, the header keeps some room free for a menu that does not exist yet.

Below 1280 pixels of width, the header has two rows: the logo above the line, the page's title below it. From 1280 pixels up, there is room for one row, and the title moves between the logo and the menu's room. This is the only change of the frame at a fixed width.

## The text grows with the page

Instead of jumping from one size to another at fixed widths, the frame's text grows on a straight line as the page widens, from a page 320 pixels wide to one 1366 pixels wide, and stays the same beyond:

- the column's text, from 18 to 20 pixels;
- the page's title, from 24 to 32 pixels;
- the footer's text, from 16 to 18 pixels.

### The headings grow with the text

The headings follow the column's text instead of a range of their own: a section heading, like the one above the list, is always 1.2 times the column's text, and a sub-section heading, like this one, 1.1 times.

## The column

The text is set in a single column, centred, at most 60 characters wide. On a narrow screen it fills the width; on a wide one it stops at its limit, and since its characters grow with the page, so does the column. Everything the text holds, headings, lists, code and tables, lives inside this column; how each of them looks is on its own page of this style guide.
