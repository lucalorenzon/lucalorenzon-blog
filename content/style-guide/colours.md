Here are my choices on the colours of this site. I love dark pages, but I know many people don't. I started, selfishly, from the dark, because I wanted to recall the first BBS terminals: amber on black. Then I recognised my egocentrism and set out to destroy it, looking for what reads better for the readers. So I decided to let the scheme follow the reader's preference. Here you find the colours chosen for both schemes, light and dark.

## Ink

This is the most used colour. As I explained above, it should have been amber on a dark background, but that was too heavy and hard to read. On the other hand, pure white smears on a dark background for readers with astigmatism. So in the dark scheme the ink is off-white, not pure white.

## Code accent

Text all in the same colour is boring: I need something that emphasises some words, so I need an accent. Because it sits inside normal-size text, it needs the same strong contrast as the ink, and in the light scheme this takes a darker shade than the other accents.

## Heading accent

Headings are an accent too. They use a larger font, so a lighter shade is enough, and this colour is never used on the code surface, where it would be too dim.

## Line accent

To keep the meaning of the accent, I have tried to keep it on lines too. Because lines are graphics, not text, a contrast of 3:1 is enough, and amber stays an accent because lines are thin.
