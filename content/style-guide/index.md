Designing a website means taking a position on a lot of questions. Often you are not sure of the choice you made, and even when you are, you still want to keep the chance to change it.

Here I have externalised that kind of decision, right or wrong as it may be. But you know what? It is exactly those decisions, right or wrong, that make this site mine and unique.

On bigger sites you would probably use a design system, with design tokens and the rest of the UX toolbox. Here, on a personal blog, that would be too much. Still, I need a place that gathers this kind of volatile decision, so that each one is tested once, and on its own.
